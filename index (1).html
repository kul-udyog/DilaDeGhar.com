About Us

Who's behind DilaDeGhar

DilaDeGhar.com is run by Kul Udyog, an independent real estate advisory working across Zirakpur, Mohali, Chandigarh, New Chandigarh and Panchkula.

We're an authorized channel partner for the projects listed on this site — not the builder, and not a large brokerage. That means our job is to give you a straight comparison across projects, not to push whichever one pays the highest commission.

Every project featured here has been personally verified before it's added to the list, and we stay involved with you from your first enquiry through site visits, booking, and possession.
