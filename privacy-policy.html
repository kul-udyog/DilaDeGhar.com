Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

DilaDeGhar.com ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Kul Udyog, an independent real estate advisory. This page explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and who we share it with.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry, request a callback, or contact us via a form on this site, we collect the details you provide, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (where provided)
  • The project or budget you're enquiring about

We do not collect payment information, government ID numbers, or any sensitive personal data through this site.

How We Use Your Information

Information you submit is used to:

  • Contact you about the project(s) you enquired about
  • Share your enquiry with the relevant developer or project team, where applicable, so they can assist with your request
  • Improve our understanding of buyer interest across the areas and projects we cover

How Your Information Is Stored

Enquiries submitted through this site are recorded in a secure, access-controlled system used internally by Kul Udyog for lead management. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Third-Party Links

This site links out to individual project websites (each independently operated). Once you leave DilaDeGhar.com, that site's own privacy policy applies to any information you share there.

Your Choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, or to delete the information you've submitted, at any time by reaching out to us using the contact details below.
