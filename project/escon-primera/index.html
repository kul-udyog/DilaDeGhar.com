FAQs

Questions buyers ask

Is Escon Primera ready to move?

Yes. Escon Primera on PR-7 Airport Road, Zirakpur is marketed as ready-to-move, with Phase 1 and Phase 2 homes. Availability changes, so call us to confirm which floors and towers are open right now.

What is the price of a flat at Escon Primera?

2 BHK homes start from ₹1.30 Cr* (super area 1,305 sq ft). 3 BHK and 3+1 BHK homes are priced on request because rates vary by tower, floor and facing. GST, stamp duty and registration are extra. Call or WhatsApp us for the current price list.

What are the flat sizes and configurations?

2 BHK at 1,305 sq ft and 3 BHK at 1,550 sq ft in Phase 1, 3 BHK at 2,050 sq ft in Phase 2, and 3+1 BHK at 2,450 sq ft in Phase 1. All sizes are super area.

Who is the developer and is the project RERA registered?

Escon Primera is developed by Malwa Projects Pvt. Ltd. The project is registered with Punjab RERA under PBRERA-SAS79-PR0852 and PBRERA-SAS79-PR0529. Please verify details on the Punjab RERA portal before you decide.

Where exactly is Escon Primera located?

On PR-7 (Chandigarh International Airport Road), Zirakpur, Punjab 140603, with quick access to Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula. The Chandigarh International Airport is about 9.6 km away.

Is DilaDeGhar.com the official website of Escon Primera?

No. DilaDeGhar.com is an independent real estate advisory and channel partner. We help you compare, shortlist and visit projects. We are not the developer, and this page is for information only.

Can I schedule a site visit?

Yes. The site office is open every day from 10 AM to 7 PM. Walk-ins are welcome, but pre-booking is recommended so a show flat slot is ready for you. Share your number and we will confirm your visit.
